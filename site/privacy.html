Privacy notice

Model submission form — eval.idrock.uz

Who is responsible

The IDROCK AI Excellence Center at New Uzbekistan University, Tashkent. Contact: idrock@newuu.uz.

What we collect, and why

Only what the submission form asks for: your name, email address, the model name, optionally your company, and any notes you write. We use it solely to review the submission and to reply to you about it. We do not use it for marketing and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Legal basis

Your consent, given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us, and we will delete the submission.

Who processes it

  • Supabase — database and serverless function hosting.
  • Telegram — a notification containing your submission is sent to the lab's private channel so we see it promptly.

Both are located outside Uzbekistan, so submitting the form transfers your data abroad.

How long we keep it

Twelve months from submission, then deletion. Submissions we act on are kept for the duration of the evaluation and for one year afterwards.

Your rights

Email idrock@newuu.uz to access, correct or delete your data, or to withdraw consent. We respond within 30 days.

What we do not collect

No analytics, no advertising trackers, no cookies beyond a single browser-local entry remembering your language choice — which never leaves your device.
